StockUp — Support

Grocery price book for iPhone

Write to us — open a support ticket on GitHub (an e-mail address will be added here shortly). We answer every message, usually within two days. Include your iOS version and what you were doing when something went wrong.

How does StockUp work?

Add the things you buy most. Each time you shop, log the price and the package size. StockUp converts every entry to a price per unit (per lb, per oz, per fl oz, per count) so a 12 oz jar and a 40 oz jar compare fairly, remembers your best price at every store, and tells you on the spot whether today's price is a real stock-up price.

What do the verdicts mean?

Stock-up price — at or within 2% of the best price you have ever logged (or below your target price). Good price — within 10% of your best. Normal price — within 25%. High price — more than 25% above your best.

Barcode scanning

Scanning looks the barcode up in the Open Food Facts database. If the product is known, the name, brand and size are filled in for you; if not, type the name once — the barcode is kept, so next time the item is recognized instantly. Scanning never fills in prices; prices are always yours.

Does my price book sync between devices?

Not yet. Version 1 keeps everything on one iPhone, which is what makes it work with no account and no server. Use CSV export to move your book to a new phone; iCloud sync is on the list for a later version.

What happens to my data if I delete the app?

It is deleted with the app. Your price book is not stored anywhere else, so export a CSV backup before deleting or changing phones. Your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) also includes the app's data.

I bought Pro and it is not showing

Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. Pro is a one-time purchase tied to that account, so it comes back on any iPhone you use with it, at no extra cost.

Do I have to pay again every year?

No. There is no subscription. The free version keeps 25 items with every feature; Pro removes the limit for a single payment.

Does the app need internet or my location?

No. Everything works offline. The only network request in the app is a barcode lookup, and only when you scan. The app never asks for your location.

Import from a spreadsheet

Export your sheet as CSV with a header row. Only the item column is required. Recognized columns: item, brand, category, barcode, store, date (YYYY-MM-DD), price, quantity, unit, pack_count, sale, note. Units can be written as oz, lb, g, kg, fl oz, ml, L, gal, qt, pt, ct or dozen.

Backup

Settings → Export CSV saves your whole price book as a file you can keep in Files, iCloud Drive or e-mail to yourself. Import it on a new phone to restore.
