StockUp — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

StockUp is a personal grocery price book. It is built to keep your data on your device. This page explains, in plain words, what the app does and does not do with information.

What StockUp collects

Nothing. StockUp has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting service and no server of its own. The items, stores and prices you enter are stored only on your iPhone or iPad, in the app's private storage. We (the developer) cannot see them.

Barcode lookup

When you scan a barcode, StockUp sends only the barcode number to Open Food Facts, a non-profit open database, to look up the product name, brand and package size. No other information about you or your price book is sent. Open Food Facts' own privacy policy applies to that request. You can always add items by typing instead of scanning.

Purchases

StockUp Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The app receives only a confirmation that the purchase is active. We never see your payment details.

CSV export and import

You can export your price book as a CSV file and share it with any app you choose (Files, Mail, Numbers, Google Sheets…). Where that file goes is up to you. Importing reads a file you pick and stores its rows on your device.

Camera

The camera is used only while the barcode scanner is open, to read barcodes. No photos or video are saved or transmitted.

Deleting your data

Delete items or stores inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything. There is nothing to delete on our side because we hold nothing.

Children

StockUp is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.
